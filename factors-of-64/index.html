About 64

A chessboard has 64 squares. 64 is both a perfect square (8 × 8) and a perfect cube (4 × 4 × 4), the smallest number above 1 that is both.

Factor pairs of 64

A factor pair is two numbers that multiply to give 64: 1 × 64, 2 × 32, 4 × 16 and 8 × 8. Each pair is a way of arranging 64 things in equal rows, so 64 tiles can be laid out as a rectangle in 4 different shapes, one of them a square of side 8.

How to find the factors of 64 by hand

Divide 64 by 1, 2, 3 and so on. Each time the division is exact, you have found two factors at once: the divisor and the answer. You can stop at 8, because 8 × 8 is exactly 64: any larger factor has already turned up as the partner of a smaller one.

Reading down the left side: 64 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2, which is written 26 with powers. It has only one prime factor, 2.

Counting the factors without listing them

Add 1 to each power in the prime factorisation and multiply the results: (6 + 1) = 7. This works because a factor of 64 can use each prime anywhere from zero times up to its full power.

The 7 factors add up to 127. Leaving out 64 itself, the rest add up to 63, which is less than 64, so 64 is a deficient number. Of its factors, only one is odd: 1.

Divisibility rules applied to 64

The quick tests agree with the factor list above.

  • By 2: yes. The last digit is 4, which is even.
  • By 3: no. The digits add up to 6 + 4 = 10, which is not a multiple of 3.
  • By 4: yes. The last two digits make 64, and 64 ÷ 4 = 16.
  • By 5: no. The last digit is 4, not 0 or 5.
  • By 6: no. A number divides by 6 when it divides by both 2 and 3. Here the test for 3 fails.
  • By 8: yes. 64 ÷ 8 = 8.
  • By 9: no. The digits add up to 10, which is not a multiple of 9.
  • By 10: no. The last digit is not 0.
  • By 11: no. Adding alternate digits from the right gives 4 and 6. Their difference is 2, which is not 0 or a multiple of 11.

HCF and LCM with 64

64 and 63. 64 = 26 and 63 = 32 × 7. The primes they share, each with its smaller power, give the HCF: 1. Every prime with its larger power gives the LCM: 4032. By division: 64 = 63 × 1 + 1; 63 = 1 × 63 + 0, so the last divisor, 1, is the HCF. Check: 1 × 4032 = 4032 = 64 × 63.

64 and 72. 64 = 26 and 72 = 23 × 32. The primes they share, each with its smaller power, give the HCF: 8. Every prime with its larger power gives the LCM: 576. By division: 72 = 64 × 1 + 8; 64 = 8 × 8 + 0, so the last divisor, 8, is the HCF. Check: 8 × 576 = 4608 = 64 × 72.

Multiples of 64

Factors divide a number. Multiples are what you get by multiplying it. The first ten multiples of 64 are 64, 128, 192, 256, 320, 384, 448, 512, 576 and 640. A number has a fixed count of factors but endless multiples.

Common questions

What are the factors of 64?

The factors of 64 are 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 and 64. There are 7 of them.

What is the prime factorisation of 64?

64 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2.

Is 64 a perfect square?

Yes. 64 = 8 × 8, so its square root is 8.

What is the greatest factor of 64 other than itself?

32. Divide 64 by its smallest prime factor, 2, to get it.
