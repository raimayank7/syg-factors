About 225

225 is 15 × 15. Because 15 is 3 × 5, the prime factors of 225 are two 3s and two 5s.

Factor pairs of 225

A factor pair is two numbers that multiply to give 225: 1 × 225, 3 × 75, 5 × 45, 9 × 25 and 15 × 15. Each pair is a way of arranging 225 things in equal rows, so 225 tiles can be laid out as a rectangle in 5 different shapes, one of them a square of side 15.

How to find the factors of 225 by hand

Divide 225 by 1, 2, 3 and so on. Each time the division is exact, you have found two factors at once: the divisor and the answer. You can stop at 15, because 15 × 15 is exactly 225: any larger factor has already turned up as the partner of a smaller one.

Counting the factors without listing them

Add 1 to each power in the prime factorisation and multiply the results: (2 + 1) × (2 + 1) = 9. This works because a factor of 225 can use each prime anywhere from zero times up to its full power.

The 9 factors add up to 403. Leaving out 225 itself, the rest add up to 178, which is less than 225, so 225 is a deficient number. Of its factors, 9 are odd: 1, 3, 5, 9, 15, 25, 45, 75 and 225.

Divisibility rules applied to 225

The quick tests agree with the factor list above.

  • By 2: no. The last digit is 5, which is odd.
  • By 3: yes. The digits add up to 2 + 2 + 5 = 9, which is a multiple of 3.
  • By 4: no. The last two digits make 25, and 25 ÷ 4 leaves remainder 1.
  • By 5: yes. The last digit is 5.
  • By 6: no. A number divides by 6 when it divides by both 2 and 3. Here the test for 2 fails.
  • By 8: no. 225 ÷ 8 leaves remainder 1.
  • By 9: yes. The digits add up to 9, which is a multiple of 9.
  • By 10: no. The last digit is not 0.
  • By 11: no. Adding alternate digits from the right gives 7 and 2. Their difference is 5, which is not 0 or a multiple of 11.

HCF and LCM with 225

225 and 216. 225 = 32 × 52 and 216 = 23 × 33. The primes they share, each with its smaller power, give the HCF: 9. Every prime with its larger power gives the LCM: 5400. By division: 225 = 216 × 1 + 9; 216 = 9 × 24 + 0, so the last divisor, 9, is the HCF. Check: 9 × 5400 = 48600 = 225 × 216.

225 and 240. 225 = 32 × 52 and 240 = 24 × 3 × 5. The primes they share, each with its smaller power, give the HCF: 15. Every prime with its larger power gives the LCM: 3600. By division: 240 = 225 × 1 + 15; 225 = 15 × 15 + 0, so the last divisor, 15, is the HCF. Check: 15 × 3600 = 54000 = 225 × 240.

Multiples of 225

Common questions

What are the factors of 225?

The factors of 225 are 1, 3, 5, 9, 15, 25, 45, 75 and 225. There are 9 of them.

What is the prime factorisation of 225?

225 = 3 × 3 × 5 × 5.

Is 225 a perfect square?

Yes. 225 = 15 × 15, so its square root is 15.

What is the greatest factor of 225 other than itself?

75. Divide 225 by its smallest prime factor, 3, to get it.
