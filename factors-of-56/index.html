About 56

56 is 7 × 8, the times table fact children miss most. It is also 2 × 2 × 2 × 7, and eight weeks contain 56 days.

Factor pairs of 56

A factor pair is two numbers that multiply to give 56: 1 × 56, 2 × 28, 4 × 14 and 7 × 8. Each pair is a way of arranging 56 things in equal rows, so 56 tiles can be laid out as a rectangle in 4 different shapes.

How to find the factors of 56 by hand

Divide 56 by 1, 2, 3 and so on. Each time the division is exact, you have found two factors at once: the divisor and the answer. You can stop at 7, because the next number, 8, gives 8 × 8 = 64, which is more than 56: any larger factor has already turned up as the partner of a smaller one.

Counting the factors without listing them

Add 1 to each power in the prime factorisation and multiply the results: (3 + 1) × (1 + 1) = 8. This works because a factor of 56 can use each prime anywhere from zero times up to its full power.

The 8 factors add up to 120. Leaving out 56 itself, the rest add up to 64, which is more than 56, so 56 is an abundant number. Of its factors, 2 are odd: 1 and 7.

Divisibility rules applied to 56

The quick tests agree with the factor list above.

  • By 2: yes. The last digit is 6, which is even.
  • By 3: no. The digits add up to 5 + 6 = 11, which is not a multiple of 3.
  • By 4: yes. The last two digits make 56, and 56 ÷ 4 = 14.
  • By 5: no. The last digit is 6, not 0 or 5.
  • By 6: no. A number divides by 6 when it divides by both 2 and 3. Here the test for 3 fails.
  • By 8: yes. 56 ÷ 8 = 7.
  • By 9: no. The digits add up to 11, which is not a multiple of 9.
  • By 10: no. The last digit is not 0.
  • By 11: no. Adding alternate digits from the right gives 6 and 5. Their difference is 1, which is not 0 or a multiple of 11.

HCF and LCM with 56

56 and 54. 56 = 23 × 7 and 54 = 2 × 33. The primes they share, each with its smaller power, give the HCF: 2. Every prime with its larger power gives the LCM: 1512. By division: 56 = 54 × 1 + 2; 54 = 2 × 27 + 0, so the last divisor, 2, is the HCF. Check: 2 × 1512 = 3024 = 56 × 54.

56 and 60. 56 = 23 × 7 and 60 = 22 × 3 × 5. The primes they share, each with its smaller power, give the HCF: 4. Every prime with its larger power gives the LCM: 840. By division: 60 = 56 × 1 + 4; 56 = 4 × 14 + 0, so the last divisor, 4, is the HCF. Check: 4 × 840 = 3360 = 56 × 60.

Multiples of 56

Common questions

What are the factors of 56?

The factors of 56 are 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 14, 28 and 56. There are 8 of them.

What is the prime factorisation of 56?

56 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 7.

Is 56 a perfect square?

No. 7 × 7 = 49 and 8 × 8 = 64, and 56 lies between them.

What is the greatest factor of 56 other than itself?

28. Divide 56 by its smallest prime factor, 2, to get it.
